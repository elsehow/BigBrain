# BigBrain (unreleased)

- **Themes, reshuffled.** Three themes now follow your system's light or dark mode, and they lead the list: **Ink**, the default (white by day, nurebairo's near-black by night, with a brighter red), **Kind of Blue** (pale paper by day, deep blue by night) and **Something's Gotta Give**, whose night is the new Hampton's Nights. Ink replaces **Follow the system**, **Light** and **nurebairo**. If you had chosen Something's Gotta Give, it now follows your system too. Two new themes, **Jodie Foster in Contact** and **A Love Supreme**, join **OG Web Blue** and the seasons: moegiiro, adzukiiro and asagiiro are now **Spring**, **Fall** and **Winter**. yamabukiiro and Phosphorus are gone; if you used one, BigBrain switches you to Ink. If you had chosen Light or nurebairo, you keep it until you pick another theme.
- With both a Claude and a ChatGPT subscription, the recommended Pilot is now Opus 5.5 instead of GPT-6 Astra. A Pilot that follows recommendations switches the next time a subscription connects, and existing conversations keep their model. A Pilot model you chose yourself is kept.
- Desktop agents on Claude or ChatGPT can search the web. The search runs through your model's own provider, on the connection the desktop already uses, so there's no extra service or key. Like reading mail, a search turns the desktop's shell off until you allow it.
- Agents can read the PDFs your notes link: a clipped PDF that arrived without its text, or a PDF attached to an email. `read_note` reads the PDF's text layer when asked, without changing what's stored. On a desktop with Claude or ChatGPT, a scanned PDF is read by the desktop's own model, page by page.
- **Nothing is lost when the firewall can't answer.** If Jev is out of credits or unreachable, every arrival (a clip, a drop, an agent's drop, mail, meetings, feeds) now waits in one queue and lands once Jev answers, instead of being refused or retried by each source on its own. A clip sent meanwhile shows **Queued** in the browser extension, not a failure. The queue is kept outside the vault, where no agent can read it.
- Quick summaries are fast again. 0.9.2 moved Quick to Claude Haiku 5.5, which always thinks before answering: summaries took about four times as long, and most note summaries ran out of room and failed. Quick recommends Haiku 4.5 again, with thinking off. If Quick follows recommendations, it switches back automatically; a Quick model you chose yourself is kept.
