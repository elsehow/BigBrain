# BigBrain (unreleased)

- **Themes, reshuffled.** Ink is the new default: white in light mode and nurebairo's near-black in dark mode, following your system. It replaces **Follow the system**, **Light** and **nurebairo**, and its dark red is a little brighter. **Kind of Blue** is new, a pale paper by day and a deep blue by night, also following your system. moegiiro, adzukiiro and asagiiro are now **Spring**, **Fall** and **Winter**. yamabukiiro and Phosphorus are gone; if you used one, BigBrain switches you to Ink. If you had chosen Light or nurebairo, you keep it until you pick another theme.
- With both a Claude and a ChatGPT subscription, the recommended Pilot is now Opus 5.5 instead of GPT-6 Astra. A Pilot that follows recommendations switches the next time a subscription connects, and existing conversations keep their model. A Pilot model you chose yourself is kept.
- Quick summaries are fast again. 0.9.2 moved Quick to Claude Haiku 5.5, which always thinks before answering: summaries took about four times as long, and most note summaries ran out of room and failed. Quick recommends Haiku 4.5 again, with thinking off. If Quick follows recommendations, it switches back automatically; a Quick model you chose yourself is kept.
