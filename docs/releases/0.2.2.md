# BigBrain 0.2.2

One screen. The graph is the ground, and everything else — the recent
list, a search, an open note — is a text tab that slides up over it. The
camera flies, and a selection has weight.

- The recent list, search results and the open note are one drawer at the
  bottom of the home screen, over the graph. A search no longer leaves
  the picture, and an open note no longer carries a second, smaller graph
  of its own: the camera goes to the note's region instead, with its
  neighbours a hover away. Back, forward and every deep link work as
  before.
- The camera flies. Selecting a row in the recent list — the pointer on
  it, or j/k — takes the camera to that note's neighbourhood with the note
  lit at the centre; clearing the selection brings it home. A keyboard
  selection holds while the pointer explores from it, so a neighbour
  under the mouse gets its own light and name beside the selection's.
- A light is a lamp. Under a selection the rest of the picture dims with
  distance rather than all at once, the lit node glows and breathes, its
  threads draw as light off it, and its neighbourhood gathers to it: the
  nodes linked to it draw a little closer and what is not linked to it is
  eased out of the way, so the threads that matter have room. Nothing in
  the layout moves; Escape relaxes it. Hub names sit on a plate so they
  read through their own fan.
- In an open note, j/k walk its links — the entity in the text lights up,
  and its node lights beside the open one in the graph, the camera
  holding both — and Enter follows. h and l go back and forward through
  the notes you have opened. A memory note (plain markdown) renders its
  body in the tab, with citations of the record as small marks, and its
  links join the same walk.
- A new arrival appears in the graph the moment it lands: a point at the
  centre, named, turning until the gardener files it. When it is filed,
  the camera flies out to frame the threads it was given.
- Chrome. One tooltip style everywhere, Escape works in every close
  button, and the settings gear stays put. The CAPTURE band under search
  results is gone; the drop zone is the way in. Phosphor's ground is a
  step lighter.

Existing vaults need nothing.
