# BigBrain 0.2.3

The gardener reads your side of a Claude Code session.

- Since August a Claude Code session has landed in the vault as a record:
  stored, searchable, never read — 473 of them in a month, with no
  assertion and no place in the picture. Now the gardener reads the turns
  you typed, and nothing else: not the assistant's replies, not what it
  read along the way (that arrives through its own door, or not at all),
  not the harness's own notices. It files what settled — a decision and
  its why, a taste, a rule, a fact you stated about yourself or someone,
  a thing shipped — and declines a session in which nothing did. A
  session with fewer than three typed turns stays a record.
- Sessions rank last, behind every meeting, clip and mail of the day.
- The stored transcript now tells the harness from you. A slash command's
  echo, a background task's report, a context summary re-fed after a
  compaction land labelled `harness:`; system reminders are cut out.
  Search is quieter for it.

Existing vaults: after the update the projection rebuilds, and every
session already landed with three typed turns joins the gardener's list —
about 230 for a month of heavy use. They show on the home screen as
arrivals waiting for the gardener, and drain last.
