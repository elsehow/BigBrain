# BigBrain 0.2.1

The home screen is the graph. This release redraws the link graph and
makes it the ground the rest of the home screen sits on.

- The graph fills the window, edge to edge and up behind the search bar.
  It is monochrome in every palette: one ink for every node, and threads
  whose brightness follows how many assertions cite a pair together,
  adding up where they cross. Nodes are pinpricks sized by how connected
  they are, and nothing is labelled until a node is lit. Dragging and
  zooming stay smooth on a large vault.
- Minimized and maximized. Minimized, the default, keeps the search bar,
  the TYPES rail and the recent list in place and fits the whole graph
  into the room between them. Maximized is the graph alone: the rest of
  the interface comes up on ⌘K, on j/k, or when the pointer reaches the
  top or bottom of the window, and Escape sends it away. The toggle sits
  where the liveness dot was; whether a Claude Code is connected is still
  shown in settings › agents. The choice is remembered per machine.
- The recent list is a translucent sheet over the graph. Hovering a node
  and changing a type filter fade rather than snap.
- The separate graph page is retired, and so are the graph's zoom
  buttons: scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click the ground to refit.
- A source whose only cited entity is you now draws as a lone point
  instead of being left out of the graph.
- Themes. Dusk, the near-black palette the graph was drawn to, ships
  built in and is the dark half of "follow the system": a dark desktop
  that made no choice moves from Zenburn to Dusk, and Zenburn stays as a
  choice. Django Smooth is replaced by Phosphor, a brighter green, and
  three bold palettes join it: Ultraviolet, Tangerine and Hi-vis. A
  stored choice of Django Smooth reads as "follow the system" until a new
  pick is made.
- Skins gain a `mark` colour, the graph's one ink; left out, it takes the
  strongest text colour.

Existing vaults need nothing. The graph lays itself out afresh on first
open, since the picture now carries edge weights.
