# BigBrain 0.10.1

- **Find the original sources behind a concept.** Selecting a concept shows the sources cited by its claims, most recently added first. Open a source in the sidebar and keep the Field in view; Back or Esc returns to the concept.
- **Source summaries arrive as you scroll.** Quick summarizes sources near the visible part of the list. The list and reader reuse the same summary for up to 90 days, refreshing sooner when the source or Quick model changes. Leaving a view drops summaries still waiting to start; an answer already underway finishes and is saved. All Quick work shares a limit of two calls at once across the app and its background work.
- **Lens ratings stay responsive while notes are evaluated.** Rate a note, change the rule, or move on without waiting for earlier model calls. Obsolete evaluations are cancelled, and older answers cannot overwrite the current review.
- **Granola syncs again, and long meetings arrive whole.** On October 20 Granola started splitting its meeting list and long transcripts into pages: syncing stopped with "Granola meeting-list format changed", and a transcript over 40,000 characters arrived cut short. BigBrain now reads every page. The next sync picks up from the last good one, and a meeting from the last two days that arrived cut short arrives again in full.
