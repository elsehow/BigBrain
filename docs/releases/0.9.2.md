# BigBrain 0.9.2

- **Choose which agents read each account.** After updating, external agents such as Claude Code and Codex start with no access to your accounts. In Settings → Integrations, each account lists Pilot and every connected client; choose who can read it. Pilot keeps reading the accounts where Live access was on. Pilot's **Read and write** also resets to Read, so Pilot can't mark Gmail messages read or unread until you choose Read and write for it again on the account card. Saved passwords and sign-ins are kept, so nothing needs reconnecting.
- **Unused connections expire after 30 days.** A client connection that goes 30 days without use stops working until you renew it. **Renew** in Settings → Connected clients keeps the same connection and access, so nothing changes in the client. When a client tries an expired connection, BigBrain shows a notice to renew it. A connection that had already gone unused for 30 days raises that notice as soon as its client starts after the update.
- **See what agents read.** Each account in Settings → Integrations has **Recent reads**: who read it, with which tool, and whether it worked. Each client in Settings → Connected clients shows its last successful read. The log stays on this computer for at least 90 days and includes search queries, but never what was read.
- **Desktops ask for the hosts they need.** When a Desktop's sandbox blocks a host one of its commands needs, the conversation names the host and why the agent wants it, with **Allow for this desktop**, **Allow for all desktops** and **Don't allow**. Wildcards and local ports are still set only in Settings → Models → Desktop network.
- **The feed shows each conversation once.** A thread, or a meeting note sent again after every edit, has one place in the feed and is judged as a whole, instead of one entry per arrival. An ask you settled somewhere else, such as in a voice note or another thread, now leaves the feed when related news comes in.
- **Email drafts no longer land.** Gmail keeps every save of a draft as a new message, and each one arrived asking you to send it. Drafts are now skipped; what you send arrives as before. Drafts already in your vault stay where they are.
- **The Field reads at a glance.** Entities are drawn in full ink and sources as faint dust, and both fade by how few mentions they have, so the well-connected parts stand out. Show sources is now on unless you turned it off. While something is selected, you can point at a dimmed entity as well as a dimmed source.
- **Notices and the update strip no longer cover controls.** Settings, a Desktop's views and the chat make room for them instead.
- **Claude Haiku 5.5 for Quick.** With a Claude subscription, Quick now recommends Claude Haiku 5.5. If you connected Claude before this update, Quick keeps Claude Haiku 4.5 until you choose Claude Haiku 5.5 for it in Settings → Models.
- **Pilot no longer sends notifications.** Pilot answers in the conversation and nowhere else. Notifications from before the update stay in the conversation as ordinary messages.
