# BigBrain 0.10.0

- **After updating, run `bigbrain migrate --yes` once.** BigBrain now reads a vault as its sources and the claims made about them, and nothing else. Migrating turns the older logs (declines, revocations, aliases, bindings, copies) into claims, imports anything in `references/`, `inbox/` or `requests/` that isn't a source yet, and removes those folders and the retired editor's pages in `entities/`. It also moves your lenses from this computer into the vault. Until you migrate, BigBrain refuses a vault that still holds the old logs or folders, and lenses don't sync. Run `bigbrain migrate` on its own first: it shows what would change and writes nothing. With `--yes` it converts, checks that nothing was lost, and commits the result as one change. If the check fails, it puts the vault back. Afterwards your Field no longer draws a reference twice, beside its own source.
- **Shared vaults are now servers.** Settings is grouped as General, Agents, Servers and System, and connected clients are now **connected agents**. A server's page shows its people and every note on it, with who added it and when. It filters to Everything or Yours, and its search matches note text, not only titles.
- **Lenses decide what you share.** A lens is a rule, plus notes you add or remove by hand, plus the servers it's shared with. In Settings › Lenses you write a rule, preview it over your whole vault, rate notes, and share the lens. A lens judges a note together with the claims the gardener drew from it, so a rule about a person finds notes that never use their name. Each server's old rule becomes a lens named after the server, keeping what is already shared. When something other than you would grow a shared lens, **Conservative** (the default) holds the additions until you choose **Looks OK**, and **Yee-haw** shares them and warns you. Choose between them in Settings › General › Sharing. What a lens shares shows in the Field feed as one row per pass; hovering the row lights what it shared.
- **Join a server from an invite.** Every server has an invite page with **Open in BigBrain**, and `bigbrain://` links open the app at Connect a server with the invite filled in. On a new Mac, first run's first screen has **Join a server**: paste the invite, and BigBrain makes a vault and joins as a reader. It asks for an AI provider only when you first add something.
- **Servers answer from a copy on this computer.** No read waits on the network: the Field, search and notes from joined servers come from a copy that syncs every 30 seconds. A server that stops syncing says when and why on its settings page, instead of quietly dropping out of the Field. If your membership is revoked, the copy is removed.
- **See a server's own Field.** Hover the gear to list your lenses and servers. Choosing a lens filters your Field to what it holds. Choosing a server draws that server's own Field, the same one every member sees. Quick summaries work there too, and draw only on that lens's or server's evidence.
- **The Field holds still.** A new source moves only itself and what it was filed under; everything else keeps its place. The same record draws the same Field on any machine. Joined servers' nodes are placed around yours without moving them. The Field also fetches only what changed, so it updates sooner.
- The pointer no longer catches dots too faint to see. From far off only the bold dots answer; zooming in brings the rest into reach.
- **Desktop permissions** (the outside folders Desktops can read) and **Always reachable** (the hosts Desktops can always reach) move from Settings › Models to Settings › General.
- The app and a server now check each other's version. If one would misread the other, syncing stops and the server page says which one to update, instead of withdrawing what you shared. A copy of your vault can no longer withdraw what the original shared.
- On servers running this version, members share which notes are copies of one document and which note an entity is, so every member's Field draws them the same way.
- Granola syncs again. Granola changed its meeting list, and syncing stopped with "Granola meeting-list format changed". Nothing was lost: the next sync picks up from the last good one.
- Old scratch folders are cleaned up: an archived conversation's after 14 days idle, and the retired workers' once. One vault held 10 GB of them. Desktops keep their worktrees.
