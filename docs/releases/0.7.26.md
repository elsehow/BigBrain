# BigBrain 0.7.26

- Pilot can test websites and local app previews in an isolated browser.
- Conversation lists refresh with much less data, while full history loads when you open a conversation. Search still finds text in unopened conversations.
- Damaged saved conversations no longer prevent the app from starting. The original records stay on disk, and the app reports which histories could not load.
- Restarting preserves conversation dates and ordering. Replacing an API key takes effect on the next turn, and opening a model menu leaves your model preferences alone.
- Graph hover stays anchored under the pointer, and Pilots remain visible while you browse notes.
- Conversations keep consistent text sizing when expanded. J/K and arrow keys scroll the transcript; Shift-J/K jumps between your messages. The Agents tab uses the shared sidebar scrollbar.
