# BigBrain 0.4.0

The graph now uses shallow depth to make important notes and their connections easier to read, with smooth motion and subtle chromatic trails across every theme.

- Central notes and memories sit in the foreground. Selecting a note brings its direct connections forward, preserves their relative importance, and lets more distant connections recede into fog.
- The camera frames the selected neighborhood. Mouse hover and keyboard highlighting reveal notes without moving the camera; hovered nodes stay under the pointer, with labels above it.
- Monochrome highlights distinguish the hovered note from the selected note. Weighted edges and GPU rendering keep dense graphs clearer and interaction responsive.
- Recent items, search results, and opened notes share a compact text drawer. Search previews are removed, and references to the vault owner's note are plain text and skipped during link navigation.

Includes the pending-node settlement and desktop viewer identity fixes from 0.3.0.
