# BigBrain 0.7.7

Combined selections now explain why the selected records belong together. When
the vault has direct relationship evidence, the briefing leads with that
relationship and preserves qualifications such as expected, proposed, or
unconfirmed. Otherwise it explains concrete shared context without inferring a
connection from a shared neighbor alone.

Relationship rows in the text tab now share the graph's additive selection
gesture. Shift-click adds the highlighted record to the current selection;
Enter selects it on its own. Shift-Enter remains the Discuss shortcut,
including while a relationship is highlighted. The HUD labels Enter as SELECT
and shows Command-O to open an original source when one is available.
